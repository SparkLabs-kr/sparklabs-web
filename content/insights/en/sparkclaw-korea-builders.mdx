> "There's no homegrown Korean AI company, is there?" "Aren't we already too late in the AI race?"

Before answering, let me share how I see competitiveness in the Agentic AI era. It rests on four things:

1. **Industry Domain Expertise** — a deep understanding of problems inside a specific industry
2. **Software Architecture Expertise** — the ability to actually build it, at scale
3. **Structured Data** — data an AI can handle reliably
4. **AI Agents** — the layer that makes all of it actually run

When these four combine organically, you get past mere AI tools to a true Vertical AI company. Let me take them one at a time.

## ① Industry Domain Expertise — "What problem are you solving?"

![Analyzing industry structure and market data](/newsroom/insights/sparkclaw-korea-builders/02.jpg)

It is the question we always ask in a first meeting at SparkLabs. Technologies change; the question doesn't.

In the AI era, though, domain expertise expands a step. I call it 'Dual Domain Expertise.'

First, Industry Domain Expertise: understanding problems deeply inside manufacturing, finance, healthcare, logistics, law, bio — being able to define what actually needs solving, and finding the root problems that create real cost, waste time, and cloud decisions.

Second, Software Architecture Expertise. Thanks to AI, an MVP now takes days. But building an MVP and scaling a company are different skills.

![MVP vs Scale Up — a diagram of the gap between validation and growth](/newsroom/insights/sparkclaw-korea-builders/03.jpg)

Designing a 5-story building and a 100-story building differ from the first line of the blueprint. You cannot run a global SaaS on a demo architecture — it looks fine standing still, then the structural limits appear the moment you grow. AI is a superb assistant, but judging which architecture stays scalable long-term still takes expertise. AI lowered the barrier to MVPs; it did not remove the barrier to scale.

## ② Structured Data — Garbage in, garbage out

![Garbage In, Garbage Out — machine illustration](/newsroom/insights/sparkclaw-korea-builders/04.jpg)

If real estate is about Location, Location, Location, startups are about Timing, Timing, Timing —

![Data, Data, Data!](/newsroom/insights/sparkclaw-korea-builders/05.jpg)

and the Agentic AI era adds one more: Data, Data, Data.

People say "data is the new oil." I agree — but you have to look one level deeper. Crude oil has no value sitting underground. It becomes valuable only when discovered, extracted, refined, and converted into energy.

Data is the same. The real questions are:

> "Is the data accurate?" "Is it current?" "Is it structured?"

An AI agent running on inaccurate or stale data reaches wrong conclusions with total confidence — which can be more dangerous than not using AI at all.

Quality data requires all three: accuracy, currency, and a structured dataset.

The real winners of the AI era will not be the companies holding the most data, but the ones that refine and structure data best — into forms an AI agent can execute on.

## ③ AI Agents — design them in as teammates

![A person and a humanoid robot working side by side over a laptop](/newsroom/insights/sparkclaw-korea-builders/06.jpg)

Vertical AI is not a single model. It is a group of AI agents.

Treating AI as a team member across planning, design, execution, and operations — and building your decision loops that way — is the real reason solo founding became possible. It is also why Korea is not "late" to the AI era, but favorably positioned.

## So — is Korea really late?

![Jimmy Kim in September 2005, leading Nexon's portal division](/newsroom/insights/sparkclaw-korea-builders/07.jpg)

A photo from my days leading Nexon's portal division — I was a lot thinner then.

I think back to my Nexon days twenty years ago.

We built games the whole world played on top of the foundation layers of Microsoft, Intel, and NVIDIA. MapleStory — Dungeon&Fighter — KartRider.

Cyworld is the same story: a social-network experience before Facebook. KakaoTalk changed how Koreans communicate, built on mobile operating systems. Webtoons follow the same pattern.

> Korea has always built world-surprising applications on top of foundation layers.

Deep industry domain expertise, global scale delivered through software architecture, accumulated domain data — shaped into Korean-style applications on each era's foundation layer. That is what Korea has done for thirty years, and the pattern is repeating now under the name Vertical AI.

## The next wave?

Historically, the discipline that mattered most in scaling a company was HR — that is where multibillion-dollar companies like Salesforce and Workday came from.

I believe scale in the Agentic AI era will be decided somewhere else:

![AI Agent Orchestration — a dashboard managing multiple AI agents at a glance](/newsroom/insights/sparkclaw-korea-builders/08.jpg)

the ability to manage AI agents — **AiR (AI Resource Management)**.

As HR managed human resources, AiR manages AI resources. As Vertical AI companies multiply, AI agents will explode in number — and the next unicorns and decacorns will come from managing them. It is one of the next waves SparkLabs is watching.
